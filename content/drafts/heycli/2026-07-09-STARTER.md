## X thread

**1/** Side project: heycli — a voice remote for Claude Code. Tiny bridge server on the Mac, iOS app over an authenticated WebSocket, on-device speech. "Switch to mnemix. List my sessions. Approve." From anywhere, hands-free. (218)

**2/** Design decision I'll defend: text-only over the wire. The phone does STT/TTS on-device; no audio ever leaves it. Latency drops, privacy is structural instead of promised, and the server stays a dumb pipe for strings. (216)

**3/** The unglamorous 80%: what happens when the WebSocket drops mid-answer. The bridge now has resume, replay, and dedup — an idempotency key per command and a pending queue on the client. A voice UI that loses your approval on a tunnel hiccup is worse than no voice UI. (264)

**4/** Multi-session is the real product. You're not talking to one agent — you're switching between several running sessions by name, like lines on a switchboard. The wake-word layer is a state machine with pause/resume/undo, not a hotkey. (233)
