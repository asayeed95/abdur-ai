## 3. Stage list (aligned to Revenue closure)

Two layers: **ingress** (how they arrived) and **Revenue closure** (how the opportunity ends).

### 3.1 Ingress / working stages

| Stage | Meaning | Exit when |
|---|---|---|
| `inbound` | Real subscribe / waitlist / beta request landed with evidence. Interest only. | Named person + enough signal to score, or suppress |
| `research` | Prospector enriching (public only). Still not a conversation. | Score complete |
| `draft_ready` | Conversation card or outreach draft exists in approval-queue. **Not sent.** | Founder A4 or discard |

### 3.2 Revenue closure stages (required end-state for opportunities)

| Stage | Meaning | Bar |
|---|---|---|
| `qualified` | Real problem + useful evidence path + plausible payment path. Person named. | ICP bar; not logo alone |
| `nurture` | Real contact, weak timing or incomplete evidence; keep warm, no send pressure | Explicit reason |
| `rejected` | Disqualified (toy, no failure class, suppression, competitor-only, etc.) | Reason + evidence |
| `pilot_ready` | Two-week audit scoped; champion; anonymized failure ready | Card for founder |
| `blocked` | External gate (no champion, legal, SSO wall for *their* product, etc.) | Blocker named |

**Mapping rule:** every row eventually carries one Revenue closure stage **or** stays in ingress with `next_action` dated. Do not leave forever-`inbound` without a weekly refresh note.

**CAL-001 platforms** (Retell, Vapi, …) stay in platform packets / refresh reports until a **person** from that company enters the ledger. Company QUALIFY ≠ contact `qualified`.
